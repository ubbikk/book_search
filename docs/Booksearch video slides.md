## Notes for Implementation

**Typography:**
- Main text: Cormorant Garamond, large, centered
- Subtext: Inter or Cormorant Garamond italic, smaller
- Chat bubbles: Inter for readability

**Animations:**
- Text: Fade in with subtle rise
- Chat bubbles: Appear sequentially (typewriter or fade)
- Buttons: Highlight on "click"
- Book covers: Slide in from right or fade with slight scale

**Transitions:**
- Between slides: Fade or subtle dissolve
- Keep it elegant, not flashy — matches the cinematic theme

**Audio (optional):**
- Subtle ambient music, warm/sophisticated tone
- No voiceover needed (text carries the message)
